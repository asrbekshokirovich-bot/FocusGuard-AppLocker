## 🇺🇿 O'zbekcha

### Hisobingizni qanday o'chirasiz
1. FocusGuard ilovasini oching
2. **Profil** bo'limiga o'ting
3. Pastga tushib **"Hisobni o'chirish"** tugmasini bosing
4. Tasdiqlang — hisobingiz va barcha ma'lumotlaringiz darhol o'chiriladi

### Qanday ma'lumotlar o'chiriladi
- Hisob ma'lumotlari (email, ism)
- Fokus statistikasi va tarix
- Rejalar va sozlamalar
- Bulutli zaxiradagi barcha yozuvlar (Firebase)

Bu ma'lumotlar **darhol va butunlay** o'chiriladi, saqlab qolinmaydi.

## 🇬🇧 English

### How to delete your account
1. Open the FocusGuard app
2. Go to **Profile**
3. Scroll down and tap **"Delete account"**
4. Confirm — your account and all associated data are deleted immediately

### What data is deleted
- Account data (email, name)
- Focus statistics and history
- Plans and settings
- All cloud-backup records (Firebase)

This data is deleted **immediately and permanently**; nothing is retained.
