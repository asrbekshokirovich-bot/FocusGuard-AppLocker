## 🇺🇿 O'zbekcha

FocusGuard ("biz") foydalanuvchi maxfiyligini hurmat qiladi. Ushbu siyosat
ilova qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi va ulardan qanday foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

**Yig'iladigan ma'lumotlar:**
- **Hisob ma'lumotlari:** ro'yxatdan o'tishda email va ism (Firebase Authentication orqali).
- **Foydalanish statistikasi:** fokus seanslari, kunlik maqsadlar, bloklash urinishlari — faqat ilova ichida statistika ko'rsatish uchun.
- **Ilovalardan foydalanish (Usage Access / Accessibility):** qaysi ilova ochiq ekanini aniqlash uchun — FAQAT siz bloklagan ilovalarni cheklash maqsadida. Ekran tarkibi o'qilmaydi. Bu ma'lumot qurilmangizdan tashqariga yuborilmaydi.

**Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish:**
- Bloklash funksiyasini ishlatish, statistika va daraja tizimini ko'rsatish.
- Bulutli zaxira (Firebase Firestore) — ma'lumotlaringizni yangi qurilmaga ko'chirish uchun.

**Uchinchi tomonlar:** Google Firebase (autentifikatsiya va saqlash). Biz
ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va reklama uchun ulashmaymiz.

**Ruxsatlar:** AccessibilityService va "Ustiga chizish" (overlay) — bloklash uchun;
"Usage Access" — chalg'ituvchi ilovani aniqlash uchun; "Bildirishnomalar" —
eslatmalar uchun; "Internet" — bulutli zaxira uchun.

**O'chirish:** Hisobingizni va barcha ma'lumotlaringizni ilovaning o'zidan
o'chirishingiz mumkin: Profil → "Hisobni o'chirish". Yoki quyidagi email orqali
murojaat qiling.

## 🇬🇧 English

FocusGuard ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the
app collects and how it is used.

**Data we collect:**
- **Account data:** email and name at sign-up (via Firebase Authentication).
- **Usage statistics:** focus sessions, daily goals, block attempts — used only to show in-app statistics.
- **App usage (Usage Access / Accessibility):** to detect which app is in the foreground — solely to restrict apps YOU choose to block. Screen content is never read. This data never leaves your device.

**How we use data:** to provide blocking, show statistics and the level system,
and back up your data (Firebase Firestore) so you can move to a new device.

**Third parties:** Google Firebase (auth and storage). We do not sell your data
or share it for advertising.

**Permissions:** AccessibilityService and Display over other apps (overlay) — for
blocking; Usage Access — to detect distracting apps; Notifications — for reminders;
Internet — for cloud backup.

**Deletion:** You can delete your account and all data from within the app:
Profile → "Delete account". Or contact us by email.
